FAQ

Simple answers for a deliberately simple service.

Is this a full website redesign?

No. SmallPatch focuses on targeted upgrades. If your menu, booking, intake, quoting, or invoicing flow is the problem, that’s what gets fixed.

Do I need to understand the software?

No. The goal is a done-for-you setup that you can actually use after handoff.

Can you build something outside the three listed services?

Yes. Lightweight forms, automations and small internal tools can be scoped separately when the use case is clear.

Why are the prices listed as “from”?

The listed Stripe checkout amounts cover straightforward setups. If your content, integrations, data import or custom workflow is more involved, confirm scope before purchasing.

Not sure which upgrade fits?

Use the short intake form to create a clean request for a custom scope. For the three standard services above, you can pay securely through Stripe and get started immediately.

No data is stored or transmitted by this page. The request is copied locally so you can send it through your preferred contact channel.
